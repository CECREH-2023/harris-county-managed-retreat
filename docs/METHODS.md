# Methods and interpretation

The project uses a prospective system-dynamics approach: stocks represent accumulated quantities or defined indices; flows change those stocks over time; auxiliary relationships connect hazard exposure, community response, funding, implementation, equity, and land restoration. Current equations and values are specifications for review, not fitted results.

The [project overview](PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md) defines the county, time, geography, and currency boundary. The [data dictionary](../documentation/Model_Data_Dictionary.csv) separates observed-data construction from simulation relationships and retains source and uncertainty information for each entry. The [source assessment](../reports/source_assessment/Variable_Source_Assessment.md) distinguishes observed inputs, proxies, assumptions, calculated outputs, and policy choices.

Calibration must match observation and model units: parcels are not households, people are not properties, fiscal balances are not disbursement flows, and projected land use is not measured restoration. Candidate historical series are distributed for future model comparison. No causal effect, predictive performance, policy ranking, or simulation outcome has been estimated in version 0.1.0.
